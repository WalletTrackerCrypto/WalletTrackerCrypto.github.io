Your data stays with you

It only reads public addresses: it never asks for your recovery phrase or private keys.

What it does

  • Stores your data encrypted on your phone.
  • Reads public blockchain and price services directly.
  • Optional PIN, hide amounts with one tap, and screenshot blocking.

What it doesn't do

  • It is not a wallet: it cannot send, receive, buy, sell or swap crypto.
  • No account, no sign-up and no servers of our own.
  • No ads and no analytics.
